Quotations from other works

Some data files of a paper folder (REUSE.toml names them) quote other works, such as papers and Wikipedia articles.
Each quotation names its source next to it. The quotations are not licensed by this repository: they remain under
their sources' terms, and are reproduced here to record where a word, letter or symbol was found. Reuse a quotation
only as its source's terms, or an exception for quotation in your law, allow.
The rest of each such file is licensed under CC BY 4.0 (LICENSES/CC-BY-4.0.txt).
